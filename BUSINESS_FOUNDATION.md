# SkyPearl Realty - Business Foundation & Operating Blueprint
**Headquarters**: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India  
**Platform**: SkyPearl Enterprise Real Estate & Lead CRM

---

## 1. Business Model & Revenue Streams
SkyPearl Realty operates as an enterprise tech-enabled real estate brokerage and asset advisory firm in Chennai, bridging high-intent buyers/tenants with verified property owners and developers.

### Core Revenue Streams:
1. **Residential Primary & Secondary Sales Commission**:
   - **2.0%** of total transaction value on standard resale / outright property sales (1% from Seller, 1% from Buyer, or 2% from Developer on new projects).
2. **High-Value Luxury & Villa Leasing (ECR, Boat Club, Poes Garden)**:
   - **1 Month Rent** equivalent as professional brokerage service fee from Landlord & Tenant.
3. **Commercial & IT Park Office Advisory (OMR, Guindy, Ambattur)**:
   - **1 to 2 Months Rent** equivalent on 3-year to 9-year commercial lease agreements.
4. **Property Management & NRI Tenant Management**:
   - 8% to 10% annual rental management fee for NRIs owning properties in Chennai.

## 4. Listing Quality, Verification & Privacy Policy
1. **Duplicate Listing Detection**: Algorithms prevent multi-agent collisions by cross-checking locality, door/building number, and floor area.
2. **72-Hour Physical Verification**: Every property receives a physical visit before receiving the "SkyPearl Verified" seal.
3. **Owner Privacy Protection**: Unverified external visitors cannot view owner phone numbers without an assigned agent mediation.
4. **Audit Trail**: Every record modification, phone number access, and deal stage change is permanently logged in the activity trail.
